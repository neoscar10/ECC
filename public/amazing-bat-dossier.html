HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

Artifact Narrative

Hand-signed in striking red archival ink upon premium light willow wood, this specimen serves as a tangible anchor to the 1992 World Cup Final. Its provenance traces back to the private collection of a senior official within the London cricketing circuit, where it remained in climate-controlled stasis for over three decades.

The signature remains vibrant, defying the typical degradation seen in artifacts of this vintage. It is not merely equipment; it is a piece of legislative history for the sport, representing the bridge between the heritage of the 20th century and the commercial explosion of the 21st.
