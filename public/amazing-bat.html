Amazing Bat

Signed memorabilia from the 1992 World Cup Final, a relic of unparalleled sporting history.

Description

This extraordinary piece of cricket history features the original willow used during the climactic overs of the 1992 World Cup. Personally signed by the winning captain, the bat carries the weight of a nation's triumph. The wood has aged to a deep, resonant ochre, and the signatures remain vivid and crisp.
